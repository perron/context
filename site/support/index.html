Context Support

Context is a native browser for iPhone and iPad. Here is the fastest way to get help.

Contact

Email karl@topcloud.com. Include your device model, iOS version, Context version, and the steps that caused the problem. Do not email passwords, API keys, or private browsing content.

Report a bug

Public, non-sensitive bugs can be filed in GitHub Issues. Security reports should follow the private instructions in SECURITY.md.

Common questions

  • Does Context include Grok or Grok Bot? No. They are separate services and may require their own accounts or subscriptions.
  • How does “Ask Grok” work? Context prepares an editable page prompt, then lets you share it through iOS, copy it, or open Grok or Grok Bot. Compatible destinations appear in the iOS share sheet. Opening another app by itself does not send the prompt.
  • Can Context post on X? Context opens X's composer with the page title and URL. You review and publish the post in X; Context never posts automatically or receives your X credentials.
  • Why did a site stop working? Open the shield menu and turn protection off for that site, then reload.
  • Where are bookmarks and history? Open the More menu, then Library. They stay on your device.
  • What devices are supported? Context 1.0 requires iOS or iPadOS 26 or later.
