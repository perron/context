Context

Privacy Policy

Effective August 23, 2026

What Context collects

Context does not collect personal information, browsing history, search history, diagnostics, analytics, or advertising identifiers. Context does not use tracking SDKs and does not create an account of its own.

What stays on your device

Bookmarks, browsing history, website data, and content-blocking preferences are processed and stored locally on your device. Deleting the app removes data stored by Context, subject to iOS behavior for app data and backups.

Grok Bot handoffs

Context can prepare a handoff containing your task, teammate label, page title, page URL, and—if you enable it—readable page text. Context does not send that handoff automatically. The handoff leaves Context only when you copy it, choose a destination in the iOS share sheet, or paste it into another service. Opening Grok Bot is a separate action and does not transfer the prepared text by itself.

Websites, Grok, and Grok Bot

When you visit a website, including grok.com, your device communicates directly with that third party. Those services may collect information under their own privacy policies. Context does not receive your Grok or Grok Bot credentials or conversations. Grok is provided by xAI; Grok Bot is provided by Cursor/Anysphere. Both are independent from Context.

Content blocking

Context applies bundled EasyList and EasyPrivacy rules on the device. It does not download executable code or send blocked-request records to the developer.

Children

Context is a general-purpose browser and is not directed to children. Because Context itself collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If Context's data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before the relevant app release.

Contact

Questions can be sent to perron@mac.com. You can also review the source code on GitHub.
