# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, specific, and useful. Assume good intent while reviewing the
work itself. Harassment, threats, discrimination, and personal attacks are not
accepted.

## Scope

This standard applies in the repository, project discussions, and public
events where a person represents Context.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to perron@mac.com. The maintainers
will review reports promptly, protect reporter privacy where possible, and
take proportionate action.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when this standard is violated.
